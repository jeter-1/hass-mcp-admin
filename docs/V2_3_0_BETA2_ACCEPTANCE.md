## Recovery and evidence limits

Local recovery is a normal revert of the bounded release/CI/correction commits
while retaining reviewed implementation commits and all evidence. A source
revert neither reverts a deployed artifact nor undoes a physical action.

Installed rollback requires a separately authorized compatible artifact, Core,
configuration and backup decision. Preserve ordinary-fan-v1 records and unresolved
holds; an older binary is not proven to interpret new 8.5.0 prepared hashes.
Never relabel or rewrite a historical receipt to make rollback appear compatible.

Prior acceptance remains bound to its original source, pairing and time.
Complete Android navigation, cache-only startup during an outage and independently
verified backup contents remain unestablished. Notification, helper/dashboard
canaries, backup, restart, Core update and provider upgrade retain separate bounds.
Publication and disposable execution alone do not establish household acceptance.
