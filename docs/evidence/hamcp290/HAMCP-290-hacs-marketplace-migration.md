### Is it one-way? What does a downgrade do?

**Inference — beta; recheck at 2026.11.0:** it is one-way in the inspected implementation: there is no reverse migration restoring `hacs` ownership, options, old storage and deleted code. Safe/recovery mode avoids the initial domain remap and legacy cleanup, but entering it **after** a normal migration does not reverse changes already persisted. A disabled entry may have only its domain remapped; a failed setup may have a mixture of changes. Do not assume any of these states equals the pre-upgrade state.
