reverted before publication without changing the accepted runtime. Installed
binary recovery requires a verified compatible artifact and retention of current
receipts, operation IDs, locks/holds and uncertain dispatch knowledge. RC2 is a
candidate recovery version only when its artifact and compatibility are verified.

older execution state merely to recover a binary; code, storage, Core rollback
and household restoration remain separate owner decisions.

Preserve beta.3's raw-catalog runtime-bracket qualification; later captures do not
repair it. Retain supplied-evidence attribution, the unknown historical upgrade
authority-exception cause, incomplete dependency/consumer coverage and dynamic
opacity, non-atomic dashboard saves, HA-state versus physical-feedback limits,
and unestablished complete Android navigation, cache-only outage startup and
