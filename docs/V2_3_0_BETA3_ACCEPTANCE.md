## Separately authorized power acceptance

Do not run household actions during source/release preparation. Prepare a
concrete proposal for an operator-designated light and switch, with current
state, target identity, consumers, disclosed consequences and an exact restoration
decision. Do not substitute another domain or create fixtures without authority.

Ordinary exact power requests use authenticated connector authorization; they
do not require a configuration plan or panel approval. Existing governed
operations retain their own authenticated approvals. A request may affect a
critical load or integration-defined group; incomplete consequence coverage must
remain visible. Resolve ambiguity before dispatch.

For each authorized transition, retain the assistant-managed operation ID,
request/task hashes, selected provider contract, attempts, intent, response and
verification evidence. Apply the exact action once. Inspect the authoritative
task promptly and independently read the target. HA state does not establish
physical operation or every downstream effect. Already-satisfied targets may
produce a truthful verified no-op; do not mislabel that as a dispatch test.

On timeout or uncertainty, reconcile the persisted task and current state.
Repeating the original ID and arguments must not dispatch again. Never generate
a replacement ID, switch providers, bypass a hold or invoke a generic service
to recover missing information. Attempt count alone does not prove dispatch.
An unresolved outcome protects its exact target while unrelated authority settles.
Restoration requires its own exact authorized request and independent readback.

Finish with an unrelated useful read and settled identity/health. Attribute
changes in power/fan tasks, plans, events, locks, holds, leases, commits, recovery
and audit failures against the baseline. Require no unexplained active work and
zero fallback. Do not repeat accepted fan/dashboard canaries merely for timestamps.

## Recovery and limitations

Local recovery is an ordinary revert of the new validation/release commits,
retaining the reviewed feature commits and evidence. A source revert neither
reverts an installed artifact nor undoes an action.

Installed rollback needs a separately authorized compatible artifact, Core,
configuration and backup decision. Preserve `ordinary-power-v1` records and
target holds; older binaries cannot reconcile this namespace. Never rewrite
historical receipts or downgrade around unresolved work.

Earlier acceptance remains bound to its original source, pairing and time.
Complete Android navigation, cache-only startup during an outage and independently
verified backup contents remain unestablished. Notification, dashboard/helper
canaries, backup, restart, Core/provider updates and physical feedback have
separate scopes. Disposable CI and publication do not establish household
acceptance.
