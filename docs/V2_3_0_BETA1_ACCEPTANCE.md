## Recovery and limits

Local release recovery is an ordinary revert of the release-preparation commit,
retaining reviewed feature commits. Installed recovery needs a separately
authorized compatible artifact/options/Core/database/backup plan that preserves
ordinary records and holds. A source revert cannot undo physical effects or
establish that an older binary safely interprets new ordinary records.

Historical acceptance retains its original pairing and observation time.
Complete Android navigation, cache-only startup during an outage and independent
backup-content verification remain unestablished. Dashboard/helper canaries,
notifications, backup, restart and Core-update work retain their separate bounds.
Neither publication nor disposable fan execution establishes household acceptance.
