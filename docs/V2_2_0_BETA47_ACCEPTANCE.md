# Engineering 2.2.0-beta.47 acceptance

Beta 47 is the successor correction for two independently observed Beta 46
HAMCP-089 failures. Unrelated helper plans retained target-capable opacity after
finite trigger, state, domain, label and collection provenance had already
excluded the helper. Exact safety-critical helper plans separately remained
non-actionable when bounded, reviewed notification display effects were treated
as unknown controls. Engineering 2.2.0-beta.46 remains advertised until a
separately authorized protected promotion. Stable v1.1.2 and the 51-tool
Engineering registration remain unchanged.

## Closed obligation outcomes

Every state-related template obligation terminates as exactly one of an exact
target dependency, complete target exclusion, proven dependency-neutral
operation, bounded target-capable opacity or coverage/processing failure. The
whole-template ledger does not infer safety from an empty direct-reference
count, a disabled automation, current runtime state or a partial candidate set.

The bounded value model retains complete finite entity candidates, candidate
completeness and overflow, exact domains, literal label identity and membership
fingerprints, fixed trigger entities, exact state-object provenance and reviewed
scalar/context types. Assignments, branches, lists, tuples, mappings, loops,
subscriptions, projected attributes and reviewed filter transport cannot discard
that provenance.

For complete state and zone triggers, `trigger.entity_id`,
`trigger.from_state` and `trigger.to_state` remain tied to the configured finite
entity set. Reading `state`, `name`, `last_changed`, `last_updated` or reviewed
context identity fields consumes that fixed state provenance and returns a typed
scalar; it does not create another entity selector. The same rule applies while
projecting finite collections with `map(attribute=...)`. Reviewed `now()`,
`as_timestamp()`, datetime subtraction, `total_seconds()`, scalar conversion,
formatting and comparison remain dependency-neutral. Feeding any resulting
runtime scalar back into `states()`, `state_attr()` or another entity selector
without complete finite identity remains target-capable opacity.

Complete `states.sensor` and `states.binary_sensor` domains exclude an
`input_boolean` target. `states.input_boolean`, a dynamic domain or an arbitrary
state collection does not. Complete literal-label membership can prove inclusion
or exclusion; dynamic, stale, malformed, failed, truncated or overflowing label
evidence remains conservative. Membership and registry-generation changes alter
the approval-bound evidence fingerprint.

Only a source with a surviving exact target dependency or genuine target-capable
opacity may attach its downstream consequence to the helper. Sources containing
only complete exclusions and dependency-neutral operations add no consequence,
risk elevation or target-specific downstream automation lock. An independent
exact structured or template dependency from the same source remains causal.

## Reviewed action-effect semantics

Action profiles continue to distinguish complete traversal, complete semantic
classification, presentation compaction, transport fragmentation and an actual
processing-limit stop. Bounded visible lists and profile fragments are not
analytical failure. Exact counts and full-set fingerprints bind action domains,
services, reason codes, effect targets, material effect data and normalized
action structure, including values beyond visible prefixes.

The reviewed direct effect domains retain their proportional consequence.
Exact lock, alarm, valve and other safety-critical actions remain high risk and
require `elevated_admin`, but are approval-actionable when their targets and all
other effects are completely classified. A targetless ordinary notification no
longer makes a separately exact physical action incomplete. A templated
notification message is display-only only when parse-only analysis proves a
bounded literal prefix prevents the result from selecting any reviewed exact
control or the `command_` and `kiosk_` control namespaces.

Dynamic-first notification content, TTS, location refresh, channel removal,
mobile commands, kiosk controls, unreviewed notification extensions, custom or
dynamically selected services, unresolved scripts/scenes and unknown transitive
targets remain semantically incomplete. No template is rendered or executed.
Unknown action behavior remains non-actionable and conservatively locked.

True structure, depth, candidate, membership, effect-data or source exhaustion
remains coverage failure. A missing profile, missing lock identity, unavailable
source or omitted material that was not completely examined and hash-bound also
fails closed.

## Risk model, approval and locks

New helper plans use `helper-dependency-risk-v6`. Persisted v3, v4 and v5 plans
remain readable with their existing hashes and historical classifications, but
require replanning and cannot authorize approval or execution. They are never
reinterpreted under v6 semantics.

Require these v6 outcomes:

- an unrelated standard-helper fixture has zero exact dependencies, zero
  target-relevant opacity, zero downstream profiles, complete evidence, no
  physical consequence, low risk, `standard_admin`, execution eligibility and
  approval actionability;
- an exact, fully classified safety-critical fixture retains every relevant
  dependency and profile, remains safety-critical/high with `elevated_admin`,
  and is approval-actionable;
- an arbitrary selector or unknown effect remains incomplete, high/elevated,
  non-actionable and conservatively locked; and
- a processing or coverage failure remains non-actionable and conservatively
  locked.

Risk assessment, approval binding, final preflight and F3 locking consume the
same immutable evidence. Exact target relationships acquire exact helper and
downstream locks. Excluded or neutral sources add no target-specific downstream
lock. Genuine opacity and coverage failure acquire the conservative dependency
guard. Changes to candidates, labels, domains, triggers, services, targets,
selectors, material action data, effect semantics or completeness reject stale
approval before dispatch; display-only metadata remains nonmaterial.

Approval authority 3, task schema 1, exact helper provider routing, durable
intent, one-attempt dispatch, authoritative reread, duplicate suppression,
readback-first uncertain-response behavior, recovery, audit attribution and
zero fallback remain unchanged. No public MCP schema, tool registration,
provider admission or write authority changes.
