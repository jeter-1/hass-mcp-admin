HAMCP-089 planning failure. A fresh Beta 47 index reported complete inventory

59 target-irrelevant opaque obligations attached six downstream profiles and

## Target-scoped obligation contract

Every persisted dependency obligation now carries one bounded,
target-independent selector scope derived from the immutable dependency-index
snapshot:

- a closed finite candidate set;
- a closed entity-domain set;
- dependency-neutral behavior;
- target-capable uncertainty; or
- a coverage/processing failure.

The helper projection combines that scope with the exact requested entity and
terminates in one of exact inclusion, complete exclusion, dependency-neutral,
target-capable opacity or coverage failure before it selects any downstream
profile. Candidate and domain values retained on a target-capable obligation
remain diagnostic only; they cannot prove exclusion. An exact inclusion plus an
unresolved remainder remains target-capable and conservative.

Fixed trigger State objects retain their configured finite entities. Reviewed
State scalar attributes, context identity and duration values do not create an
entity selector. Their runtime value can remain unknown without making every
helper relevant. If such a scalar is later supplied to `states()`,
`state_attr()` or another entity-selector operation, the selector remains
target-capable opacity unless finite identity is separately proven.

Complete literal-label membership, finite candidates and closed domains remain
fingerprint-bound. Failed, stale, malformed, truncated, overflowing or dynamic
evidence stays conservative. No template is rendered or executed, and no
helper, automation or household identity is a semantic exception.

## Consequence, approval and lock agreement

Only a source retaining exact target inclusion, genuine target-capable opacity
or target-relevant coverage failure may attach its downstream profile. A source
whose relationships are all complete exclusions or dependency-neutral cannot
contribute consequence, risk elevation, approval friction or a target-specific
downstream automation lock. An independent exact relationship in the same
source remains causal.

Risk, physical consequence, approval actionability, evidence fingerprints,
final preflight, F3 locking and persisted observability consume the same
target-scoped projection. Candidate, label, domain, trigger, selector, profile
effect or completeness drift changes the approval-bound fingerprint and rejects
stale execution before dispatch. Configuration operations acquire exact helper
locks for finite inclusions, the conservative guard for target-capable or failed
evidence, and no helper-dependency lock for proven unrelated evidence.

New plans use `helper-dependency-risk-v7`. Persisted v3, v4, v5 and v6 evidence
remains readable with its historical hashes and classification, but requires a
fresh v7 plan and cannot authorize approval, current lock projection or
dispatch.
